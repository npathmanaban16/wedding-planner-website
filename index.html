About

Designing Dreams,
One Celebration at a Time

Hey there! I'm Astrid — a destination wedding planner based in Bussigny, near Lausanne. I love being able to share in the love of two individuals and feel blessed to get to work with such wonderful people each day.

With over 18 years of experience, I spent 12 years in Italy as an independent wedding planner, organizing beautiful celebrations on Lake Como, Lake Maggiore, and in the Alps. Seven years ago I moved to Switzerland and had the opportunity to become the Wedding & Events Manager at the iconic Fairmont Montreux Palace, where I orchestrated countless luxury weddings — including elaborate multi-day Indian celebrations rich in vibrant cultural traditions.

My background in hospitality from EHL — the world's number one hospitality management school — and a foundation in design from the Politecnico di Torino allow me to bring together creativity, precision, and a deep love for beautiful celebrations. I handle every detail so you can be fully present in every moment.

Services

Tailored to You

Full Planning & Timeline

From the initial consultation to post-wedding follow-up — I manage budgets, timelines, contracts, and vendor coordination so you don't have to worry about a thing.

Venue & Vendor Curation

From the Fairmont Montreux Palace to Lake Como villas and alpine châteaux — I'll find the perfect venue and connect you with the finest local vendors.

Design & Décor

Florals, tablescapes, lighting, stationery — every visual detail is thoughtfully curated to create a cohesive, enchanting atmosphere that reflects your story.

Wedding Day Coordination

Rehearsal, ceremony, and reception — I ensure every moment of your big day runs flawlessly, handling any last-minute details and problem solving behind the scenes.

Destination Weddings

Switzerland, Italy, or anywhere in Europe — with 18 years of experience across borders, I coordinate every logistic for a seamless destination celebration.

Multi-Day & Cultural Celebrations

Experienced with elaborate multi-day celebrations including Indian weddings and culturally rich traditions — welcome dinners, ceremonies, and post-wedding events all beautifully orchestrated.
