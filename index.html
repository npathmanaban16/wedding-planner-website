About

Designing Dreams,
One Celebration at a Time

With a passion for beauty and an eye for detail, I transform your vision into an unforgettable celebration. Based in the heart of Switzerland, I bring together the finest venues, artisans, and experiences to create weddings that are as unique as the love stories they celebrate.

Every couple deserves a wedding that feels authentically theirs — from intimate elopements in the Swiss Alps to grand lakeside celebrations. I handle every detail so you can be fully present in every moment.

My approach is deeply personal. I take the time to understand your story, your style, and your dreams, then weave them into a seamless, breathtaking day.

Services

Tailored to You

Full Planning

From the first spark of inspiration to your final dance, I'll manage every element of your wedding with meticulous care and creative vision.

Venue Curation

Switzerland offers breathtaking venues — alpine châteaux, lakeside estates, historic hotels. I'll find the perfect setting for your story.

Design & Styling

Florals, tablescapes, lighting, stationery — every visual detail is thoughtfully curated to create a cohesive, enchanting atmosphere.

Day-of Coordination

For couples who love to plan but want a seasoned professional ensuring everything runs flawlessly on the big day.

Destination Weddings

Dreaming of a Swiss destination wedding? I coordinate every logistic — from guest travel to local customs — for a seamless experience.

Luxury Experiences

Welcome dinners, post-wedding brunches, curated guest experiences — I craft a complete wedding weekend for you and your loved ones.
