About Astrid

A passion for creating unforgettable moments

Bonjour — I'm Astrid. Based in the heart of Switzerland, I have spent over a decade turning couples' visions into beautifully orchestrated realities. Whether you dream of exchanging vows beneath the snow-capped Alps, on the shore of Lake Geneva, or in a Zurich city garden, I am here to make it effortlessly yours.

My approach is deeply personal. I take the time to truly know you as a couple — your story, your aesthetic, the little details that matter most — so that every element of your day feels authentic and intentional.

Fluent in English, French, and German, I work seamlessly with clients and vendors across Europe, ensuring a smooth experience no matter where you or your guests are coming from.
