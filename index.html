About

Designing Dreams,
One Celebration at a Time

Based in Bussigny, near Lausanne, I am a luxury wedding and event planner specializing in destination weddings across Switzerland and Europe. With a background in hospitality from EHL — the world's number one hospitality management school — and a foundation in design from the Politecnico di Torino, I bring together creativity, precision, and a deep love for beautiful celebrations.

Having honed my craft as Wedding & Events Manager at the prestigious Fairmont Montreux Palace, I know what it takes to orchestrate flawless events in Switzerland's most extraordinary settings — from the shores of Lake Geneva to the peaks of the Swiss Alps.

My approach is deeply personal. I take the time to understand your story, your style, and your dreams, then weave them into a seamless, breathtaking day. Every couple deserves a wedding that feels authentically theirs, and I handle every detail so you can be fully present in every moment.
